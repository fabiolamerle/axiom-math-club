About AXIOM

Our mission

To promote academic excellence and mathematical proficiency among AFROTC cadets through collaborative learning, peer mentorship, and problem solving activities.

  • Collaborative learningCadets work through problems together instead of alone.
  • Peer mentorshipCadets who are strong in math tutor other cadets, one-on-one.
  • Problem solvingClub meetings and activities built around solving real problems.

Who we are

AXIOM Math Club is a math club for AFROTC cadets. Our tutors are cadets too: they know the schedule, the pressure and the classes, because they have been through them.

Any cadet can book a free session with a tutor, in person or online, for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours. Whether you're stuck in Precalculus or getting ready for Calculus, you don't have to do it alone.

We also meet as a club to work through problems together. Check the calendar for the next meeting.

What does AXIOM mean?

ax·i·om

/ˈak-sē-əm/ · noun

A statement accepted as true without proof, used as the starting point for reasoning. Every theorem in mathematics is built on axioms.

Example, from Euclid“Things which are equal to the same thing are also equal to one another.”
if a = c and b = c, then a = b

That's the idea behind the club: build a solid foundation first, and everything else builds on top of it.
